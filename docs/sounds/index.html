Panel sounds

Regenerate with python3 scripts/generate-sounds.py. Current = exact replicas of public/panel.js at their real (quiet, gain 0.05–0.12) levels — turn the volume up. Proposed = candidates, rendered at comparable levels. Events that make no sound today: arm confirmed, armed, disarm success, wrong PIN, lockout, arm refused.
